Multi-Year Breakout Scan

Stocks breaking out above their 3-year, 5-year or 10-year closing high, with volume confirmation. Clearing a multi-year high is one of the strongest trend signals there is. Use the lookback and volume controls to tune how strict the scan is.

How the signal is computed
For each symbol with enough history:
• Prior high — the highest close over the trailing window (756 / 1260 / 2520 bars, at ~252 trading days a year).
• Broke out — the latest close is above that prior high.
• Freshness — the cross happened within the last Crossed within bars, not months ago.
• Volume — the breakout bar's volume against the 50-bar average before it. At or above Min volume × is tagged VOL ✓; below it the row shows LOW VOL.
• Or near — rows are also kept when price sits within Or within% of the multi-year high, flagged NEAR — often the best setups, still basing under resistance.
• Long history — LONG means the symbol has more bars than the window, so this is a genuine N-year high and not just an all-time high over a short listing history.
